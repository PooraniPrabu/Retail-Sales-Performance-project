# Retail Sales Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** developed using the **Superstore dataset** to analyze retail sales performance, profitability, customer segments, product performance, regional trends, shipping modes and future sales trends.

The project transforms retail transaction data into business-focused insights to support sales and profitability analysis.

---

## Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance
- Identify high-performing categories and products
- Compare sales across regions and customer segments
- Identify loss-making products
- Analyze sales by shipping mode
- Understand historical sales trends
- Forecast future sales trends

---

## Tools & Technologies

- Power BI Desktop
- Power Query
- DAX
- Superstore Dataset
- Data Cleaning & Transformation
- Data Visualization
- Business Intelligence

---

## Key Business Insights

The dashboard helps identify:

- Major sales-contributing categories and segments
- High-performing regions and products
- Products generating negative profit
- Shipping modes with the highest sales
- Category and sub-category sales distribution
- Historical and projected sales trends
- Areas requiring profitability improvement
